Risk Scoring Algorithm

Important: These numbers are this prototype's prioritization algorithm. They are not an official clinical scoring system.

Risk Score = Medical Risk + Visit Urgency + Missed Visit + Vulnerability + Follow-up Risk (capped at 100)
